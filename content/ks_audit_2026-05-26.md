actual K counts from MLB Stats API gameLogs and append a delta column.

**What we're auditing**: model's L7 K/start projection vs the pitcher's
actual strikeouts. The "Our line" column is what `generate_props.py`
suggests as a safe Over line (floor(proj) - 0.5). The "Book lean (guess)"
column is what books typically post for that K projection band.

After enough samples (~30 starters), we'll know:
- Avg miss between projection and actual
- Whether our line-suggestion floor is too conservative
- Which projection bands (>=6, 4-5, <3) are most/least reliable

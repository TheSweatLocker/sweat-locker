## Verified, not assumed

## Found, not fixed

- **`game_id` cannot join context to results in NFL and NCAAB.** Context
  keys on an md5 hash; results use a composed slug. A game_id join returns
  0 of 316 (NFL) and 0 of 1131 (NCAAB), silently. Join on resolved names
  with a +/-1 day tolerance. Needs an audit of which production readers do
  this join.
- **182 duplicate POTD receipts** — 91 identical VOID rows on 2026-07-21 and
  91 on 2026-10-16 from a bad backfill. Harmless to the record, but any
  count must drop them. Real graded POTD set: 96W-80L-5P.
- The DB `COMMENT ON COLUMN signal_registry.price_breakeven_pct` still says
  "else the per-market median fallback", which `ca98f265` made wrong.
- Price coverage, not the reader, is what blocks the price bar: only 23
  live-sport cells have a genuinely measured bar.

## The caps now yield to a floor (`c4759ad5`)

Andy, an hour after the DotD/prop caps shipped: *"I dont wnat it to be
short forever we will asses how short but we cnat just cit it off
either."*

He was right. Measured over the 31 published cards since 10-26, the caps
alone move the median card 5 -> 4 but put **ten of 31 days at 1-3 picks**.

Three things were being discarded; all three are now a bench the floor
refills from:

| was thrown away | why it is the honest first refill |
|---|---|
| `picks[:8]` dropped the tail of `curate_top_8` | slot 9 cleared the *same* 30d gate, odds band, juice gates and tier rules as slot 8 -- it is not a refused pick |
| the fade loop broke out at `FADE_CARD_MAX` | fade is the one surface we publish that makes money (+6.26%, n=139) |
| the DotD item was only *built* inside the include branch | so there was nothing left to put back |

That overflow point is what makes the floor safe where the
`MIN_CARD_PICKS` window loop is not: the loop reaches for props the 30d
gate **refused**, the floor only for picks that already **passed**.

**The ladder is two-stage, and that is the load-bearing part.** A flat
floor of 5 pads a 3-pick night straight back up with the props and DotD
just removed -- the 48.1% card we started from. So fade/overflow fill to
`CARD_FLOOR` (5); prop/DotD only to `CARD_FLOOR_WEAK` (3). A losing
surface may *rescue* a tiny card and never *pads* a merely short one.

Backtest over those 31 cards, weak rungs only (nothing was benched before
today, so this is the conservative case):

| | n | record | hit | units | ROI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| published | 156 | 74-82 | 47.4% | -24.36u | **-15.62%** |
| new rules | 135 | 70-65 | 51.9% | -10.83u | **-8.02%** |

It keeps 135 of 156 picks and the median card stays at 5. **In-sample** --
the surface ROIs that order the ladder were measured on this same window,
so +7.6pp is an upper bound, and **-8.02% is still a losing card**.

## 46 wagers were in the record twice (`db3d771f`)

Andy: *"fix and ddeup. fix root cause."* Both done. 48 extra **graded**
rows voided, every one confirmed by readback, 0 failed, 0 refused.

**The root cause was not the card.** `public_receipts` already had a
first-write-wins screen, written for exactly this. It could not see these
rows for four separate reasons:

1. `_bet_key` returned `None` whenever `game_id` was NULL -- documented as
   "a parlay slip". True of `ledger`, **false of the Sweat Card**: 284 of
   the 400 newest `sweat_card` receipts have a NULL game_id, because the
   potd, dotd, prop and MLB-era total rows all do.
2. the copies disagree on `market` -- `potd` beside `total`, `ml` beside
   `dotd`. A slot name is not a market; same defect as the card generator.
3. **VOID rows were skipped**, which licensed an endless re-mint. One
   wager is in the table **88 times**; 28,950 duplicate rows overall. It
   also makes a dedupe impossible to land -- voiding a duplicate is *how*
   the record gets corrected, and the next capture writes it back.
4. `backfill_public_receipts.py` -- nightly, 14-day window, reconstructs
   from card blobs with `game_id=None` -- had its **own copy** of the
   screen and kept a two-value unpack of a helper returning four. Its own
   comment warns about that exact failure. It made every duplicate.

**Fixed at the chokepoint:** one `bet_identity` and one
`screen_for_write`, called by both scheduled writers, with a *named*
result so a stale positional unpack raises instead of drifting. It
resolves matchup -> game_id across the live rows and the payload first,
and dedupes within a payload too -- ids 810367/810368 went in on one
batch at one `published_at`, and a guard that only reads the database
cannot catch its own caller.

**What it refuses to decide:** 2026-10-03 published two `Under 7.0` bets
on *different games*, both with NULL game_id. A row with no game_id and
no matchup is judged only when the live side holds exactly one game for
that label; with two it is written and flagged, because refusing would
erase a real wager.

**The card was being flattered.** Six of the 10 graded rows removed from
the card window since 10-25 were wins:

| | n | record | hit | ROI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| as published | 160 | 77-83 | 48.1% | -14.16% |
| deduped | 150 | 71-79 | **47.3%** | **-15.86%** |

`prop_jerry` and `sharp_card` are unchanged in that window -- their
duplicates were September.

**Also found, pre-existing and live:** `_side_for_receipt` scored its
"whole word elsewhere" branch with `rf'{team}'` written as two
**literal backspace bytes (0x08)**, so it could never match and the
function resolved a side only when the label *starts* with the team name.
A regex that cannot match is invisible on read.

Kept the **priced** copy, not the earliest -- `pick_odds` is set-once, so
an unpriced survivor can never be graded for ROI (06-20 and 07-31 had
`odds=None` on the earlier copy). VOID not DELETE, because the identity

Pinned by `test_receipt_bet_identity.py` (25 cases) in

It never runs `--apply`.


## Correction: two ROI figures above are overstated (my error)

The helper in my scratch analysis scripts computed a plus-money win as
`100/odds` instead of `odds/100`. A +150 win pays 1.50u, not 0.67u, so
every figure I derived **this session** understated returns in proportion
to how much plus-money the surface carries. The negative branch was
right, which is why it survived several passes: the error is invisible on
any favourite.

**Production code was never affected** --
`compute_surface_records._american_win_payout` and
`aggregate_daily_records._american_payout` both use
`(o/100) if o >= 100 else (100/abs(o))`. The surface ROIs already in the
code constants and comments (fade +6.26%, dawg -26.05%, prop_jerry
-13.09%, game_read -3.17%, sharp_card +0.45%) came from the earlier
analysis, which used the right formula, and are correct.

Corrected, with every conclusion unchanged:

| figure | as stated | corrected |
|---|---|---|
| card backtest, published | 47.4% / -15.62% | 46.6% / **-14.65%** |
| card backtest, new rules | 51.9% / -8.02% | 50.4% / **-8.26%** |
| dedupe, as published | 48.1% / -14.16% | 48.1% / **-11.58%** |
| dedupe, deduped | 47.3% / -15.86% | 47.3% / **-13.12%** |

Hit rates were never affected. The floor still improves the card by about
**+6.4pp ROI and +3.8pp hit rate**, and the dedupe still moved the card
*down* -- it was being flattered. The backtest n also fell, 156 -> 146,
because the dedupe has since removed the duplicate rows it was originally
measured on.

**It also killed a finding before I reported it.** The wrong formula made
plus-money picks look catastrophic: the >+100 band read **-24.33%**.
Corrected it is **-7.63%** (n=624, 41.2%) against **-4.63%** for the
-129..+100 band (n=3028). Worse, unremarkably so, and nothing like a
rule.

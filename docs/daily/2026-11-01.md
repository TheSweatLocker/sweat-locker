  +30.3%** — but **37 of 38 are NCAAF**. NFL rl/spread is **n=1** live.
  The NFL claim rests on the n=107 backtest, not on a live NFL record.

  no engine POTD.

  countable, excluded from the case above.

  games** is a constant, i.e. that field carries no injury information.
  Eyeball inactives before posting.

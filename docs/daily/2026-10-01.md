### Held, not shipped — blocked on puck-line prices

Those clear the script's gate legitimately (it IS baseline-relative: `ml 54.5`,
`rl_home 34.8`, `rl_away 65.2`, requires `lift >= 4.0`). But a lift in hit-rate

and **nowhere store the puck-line PRICE** — `close_puckline_home` is also the
line, and `line_history.price` has no NHL puck-line rows. A 72% hit on +1.5 at
an unknown -250/-400 can still lose: the artifact that made NCAAF COVERAGE read
60.5% at negative ROI.

**The unlock is ingesting puck-line odds — data acquisition, not tuning.**

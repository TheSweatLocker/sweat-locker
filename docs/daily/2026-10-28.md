## 1. FIXED — NBA spread reads were carrying moneyline prices

**15 of 29 NBA `rl` reads** held a price impossible for a spread:
`San Antonio -18.5 @ -1800`, `Boston -14.5 @ -750`,
`Philadelphia -12.5 @ -669`.

**It was not the data source and not the pricer.** `line_history` holds 427
quotes for `spread/home/-12.5` on that Philadelphia game, all between −110
and −118. `nba_game_context` separates `close_spread_home_price` from
`home_ml_close` correctly. `price_one()` maps `rl → spread` and has no
moneyline fallback.

**The cause:** the read was priced as a MONEYLINE, then regenerated as a
SPREAD, and the price survived. −669 is the median of that game's home
moneyline (which ran −850..−721, `price_best` −620). The number was right
for the market it was computed against, and the market changed underneath
it.

Evidence, re-tested on the corrected population:

```
implausible price      regenerated AFTER pricing: 23 of 23  (100%)
plausible  [CONTROL]   regenerated AFTER pricing: 148 of 361 (41%)
23 of 23 implausible prices sit in moneyline range
```

Regen is common, so regen alone proves nothing — but an impossible price
**always** followed one. That is why the fix gates on the market actually
changing, not on a timestamp.

* **Writer fixed** (`d626e01d`): `sync_jerry_reads_from_ctx.py` rewrites
  `call_market`/`call_side`/`call_line` on upsert and never touched the
  price. It now reads the old values back and NULLs the six price columns
  when the pick changes, logging it. `price_picks.py` re-prices a NULL.
* **23 stored rows cleared** (`42038005`), 0 failed, 0 remaining.

**I nearly destroyed 32 correct prices.** My first band was a flat
−140..+120, which flagged 55 rows. MLB's `rl` is a RUN LINE and NHL's a
PUCKLINE, both fixed at ±1.5, so they price far wider than a football or
basketball spread. Measured p1..p99 from `line_history`:

```
NCAAF  -122 .. +101        NHL  -285 .. +230   puckline
NFL    -121 .. +102        MLB  -235 .. +190   run line
NBA    -120 .. -101        NCAAB -118 .. +100
```

Real count with per-sport bands: **23 — NBA 15, NCAAF 8, and ZERO for NHL,
NFL and MLB.** All 28 NHL rows I first called broken were ordinary puckline
prices.

**Impact:** only 23 of 21,592 receipts were affected, worth **−0.07 units**
against the published record. The damage was to engine-side figures — NBA
spread ROI was computed against moneyline prices and is measurable for the
first time today.

## 2. NOT A BUG — NBA conviction 0 on 36 reads

I reported this as "34 of 106 reads at conviction 0, including both PRIME

```

call_text present:     0 of 36
NBA receipts produced: 0 of 36

```

They are void placeholders that never reach a user. Conviction 0 on a
no-play is correct.

**The real finding is narrower and worth keeping:** 3 games the engine
scored PRIME (82) or STRONG (78) produced **no pick at all** and were
voided — GSW @ LAL 10-13, DAL @ HOU 10-11, TOR @ DET 10-16. All in NBA's
first week, so most likely an early-season data gap in the pick logic
rather than a scoring fault. Worth re-checking after a few more weeks; if
it recurs in November it is a real hole.

## 3. FIXED — a dead NBA pace signal

`nba_pace_both_slow_under` required `home_pace <= 96 AND away_pace <= 96`.
That fires on **0 of 147** games — league p10 is 96.5, so the rule was
unreachable. It sat enabled at weight 0.3 with `sample_n=0` all season.
Threshold moved to 98 (the observed p25), which fires on 10% of games.

Direction deliberately **not** changed. The 50-game sample leans UNDER on
fast pace (64%, n=25) which argues the opposite of the existing
`nba_pace_both_fast_over`, but CI[20,55] contains 50% and flipping a side
on that is the move that has cost money before.

Four of the six NBA pace signals **cannot fire at all**: they read a
prop-level `signals.pace` dict, and NBA props do not publish (0 prop
receipts against 8,760 graded props). Not fixable without wiring NBA props,
which models at −3.85% best case — so leave them dead rather than ship a
losing surface to feed them.

## 4. MEASURED — pace, road offence and back-to-backs

Full detail in `project_nba_signal_candidates_measured_1027`. Short version
on two seasons of data (2,285 regular-season games with a close):

* **back-to-backs are fully priced** — either team on a B2B goes OVER 50.5%
  (n=743) against a 52.2% control (n=1,516). Raw effect is 0.8 points and
  the close already carries it.
* **top road offences are priced almost perfectly** —
  `corr(away ppg-prior, total − close) = +0.006` on n=2,087.
* **the interaction looked real and failed split-half** — top away offence
  plus a B2B went OVER 60.4% (n=139) but 50.0% in 2024-25 against 64.9% in
  2025-26. Not shipped.
* **pace is genuinely too early** — 50 games, and the direction is UNDER
  (36.0% OVER above median vs 48.0% below). Needs 266 games per arm;
  answerable around 2027-01-03. It is in the engine now at weight 0.242
  and climbs on its own as games accrue.

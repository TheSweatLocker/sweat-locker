### The finding

Long is a coin flip and stays banned.

**It concentrates on our own conviction.** The Sharp drops LEAN on football, so
every short spread reaching it is STRONG+ — and that is the strong subset:

- short + `tier=STRONG`: ours 4-18 → **flipped 18-4, 81.8%, n=22, CI[61.5, 92.7]**
- short + `tier=none`: flipped 50-31, 61.7%, n=81, CI[**50.8**, 71.6] — does *not*
  clear breakeven 52.4

So the tier gate **stays**. It is what selects the subset the flip works on.
The more confident the engine is on a short football spread, the more wrong it
is — same animal as `project_nfl_curated_spread_is_the_leak_1018`.

### What the implementation had to get right

1. **The flip runs last**, after every existing gate. The no-edge gate matches
   on `pp['sub']` and `_lr_shadow_conflict` compares `pp['side']` — flipping
   first would blank the refusal text and invert the shadow gate. The 81.8% was
   measured on picks that *passed* those gates.
2. **The price is posted, never synthesised.** `_spread_prices` carries the
   price of the side the *read* took. New `_flip_prices` reads `line_history`
   (one row per book/side/line, real American price), requires 2+ books on the
   exact flipped line, and the caller **drops the pick** otherwise. One request
   per sport per run, from a pre-pass — not the per-row shape that caused the
   10-21 outage.
3. **Grading needed no change — but that was confirmed first.**
   `grade_game_market_receipts` reads the receipt's own `pick_side`/`pick_line`.
   `feedback_grade_the_side_we_published` is this exact trap (641 of 692 fade
   receipts named the side we did not take).

### Verified on live rows

## B151 — The Fade was publishing a market it never validated (`0fde5772`)

The surface exists because of **one** measurement: fade heavy public on
**football spreads**, +12.4% on n=107. Of the 27 receipts it has written,
**26 are totals.**

Re-measured the live feed (`line_snapshot`, deduped to the last snapshot per
sport/game/market exactly as `build_plays` does, graded off `*_game_results`,
fade = opposite the public, at the live ≥65% threshold):

| bucket | record | hit | ROI | n | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NCAAF/NFL spread | 57-39 | **59.4%** | **+13.4%** | 96 | the edge, reproduced |
| MLB total | 130-151-15 | 46.3% | **−11.7%** | 281 | **banned** |
| NCAAF total | 86-84 | 50.6% | −3.4% | 170 | **banned** |
| NBA total | 3-3 | 50.0% | — | **6** | tracking |
| NHL total | 5-5-1 | 50.0% | — | **10** | tracking |

Totals pooled: −8.4% on n=467, negative at **every** threshold 50→80 (49.6,
48.2, 48.0, 47.6, 48.7, 45.2), early half −18.3% vs late +1.1%.

**The pooled number is not a verdict on any one sport.** Money flow inverts by
sport, so the two halves need opposite treatment:

- **MLB + NCAAF totals** — measured, negative, real n. Banned.
- **NBA + NHL totals** — *unmeasured*, n=6 and n=10, both leagues just opened.
  Banning on n=6 is the mirror of publishing on n=6. They stay, labelled
  `tracking`.

Every play now carries `evidence: validated | tracking`, so a totals pick
can't borrow the football-spread number's credibility on the same tab.

### Two things I deliberately did NOT do

**Didn't fill `_HOME_HANDICAP_SIGN` for NBA/NHL.** Nine such spreads were
dropped today as "orientation unverified". Filling it would hand the surface a
*third* unvalidated market — the exact thing B151 opened about. The sign has to
be derived from results first: `corr(close_spread, actual home margin)` is
**+0.324 on NFL** (positive = home favoured → `home_handicap = −close_spread`)
and **−0.806 on NCAAF** (positive = home handicap → `+1`). That independently
reproduces the production map. NBA has 22 final games (too few); 
`nhl_game_results` has no `close_spread` at all.

**Didn't claim the surface was refusing football spreads.** It wasn't — fade
receipts begin **10-19**, and 10-19→10-23 were Mon–Fri with no Saturday slate.
The one football day in that window (10-20 MACtion) *did* publish a spread. The
ban was still needed, because the totals it filled the week with are measurably
negative in two of four sports.

### Scoping caveat, stated plainly

The +13.4% reproduction is **NCAAF-only**. `nfl_game_results` and
`nfl_game_context` share **zero** game_ids (272 each, 0 overlap), so the 13
qualifying NFL spread splits couldn't be graded by this path at all. NFL stays
in `FADE_VALIDATED` on the strength of the 10-19 work, but this run did not
independently confirm it → **B160**.

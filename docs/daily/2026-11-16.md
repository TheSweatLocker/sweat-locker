## 3. The NBA prop engine caps at LEAN on purpose, and LEAN is UNDER-only

34,751 NBA props all-time: `LEAN 7846 / COVERAGE 22870 / SKIP 4035`. **No
PRIME, no STRONG, no LIGHT, ever.** NFL over the same table family produces
all six. This is deliberate and documented at `nba_prop_gate.py:36`:

> No PRIME and no STRONG anywhere, because **no cell measured profitably**.
> Assigning a strong tier off a projection before outcomes justify it is
> exactly how NFL ended up defaulting every edge-detected prop to STRONG.

The gate is `d == 'under' and ratio >= 1.07 -> LEAN`, so **every NBA LEAN prop
is an UNDER** — confirmed 1,923 of 1,923 in the window. Last week that cell
went 876-758 (53.6%) for **-6.3% ROI at real book odds**: the under juice eats
the whole edge. 53.6% needs better than -115 to profit and the board is paying
about -128.

So the gate is working as designed and the design is telling us NBA props
still have no profitable cell. That is the finding, not a bug.

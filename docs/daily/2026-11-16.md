## 3. The NBA prop engine caps at LEAN on purpose, and LEAN is UNDER-only

34,751 NBA props all-time: `LEAN 7846 / COVERAGE 22870 / SKIP 4035`. **No
PRIME, no STRONG, no LIGHT, ever.** NFL over the same table family produces
all six. This is deliberate and documented at `nba_prop_gate.py:36`:

> No PRIME and no STRONG anywhere, because **no cell measured profitably**.
> Assigning a strong tier off a projection before outcomes justify it is
> exactly how NFL ended up defaulting every edge-detected prop to STRONG.

The gate is `d == 'under' and ratio >= 1.07 -> LEAN`, so **every NBA LEAN prop
is an UNDER** — confirmed 1,923 of 1,923 in the window. Last week that cell
went 876-758 (53.6%) for **-6.3% ROI at real book odds**: the under juice eats
the whole edge. 53.6% needs better than -115 to profit and the board is paying
about -128.

So the gate is working as designed and the design is telling us NBA props
still have no profitable cell. That is the finding, not a bug.

Headlines:

- **The calibration loop was dead for three sports.** `backfill_signal_tiers.py`
  (nightly, `--sport ALL`, runs AFTER refit and upserts the same key) read
  **0 games** for NBA, NCAAB and NFL and then seeded every signal
  `UNVALIDATED weight 0.3`, erasing what refit had computed correctly.
  Causes: `SPORT_TABLES` had no `'NBA'` key and silently fell back to the MLB
  tables; NCAAB and NFL join context to results across two different
  `game_id` spaces. The join is now **NBA 253/264, NCAAB 505/644, NFL
  129/133**, and the seed branch refuses to write when the context table has
  rows but none joined. **346 registry rows across five sports now carry a
  measured tier.**
- **Edge basis changed live (Andy's call).** Promotion is now measured
  against the PRICE breakeven; demotion still uses the side baseline, because
  ANTI_VALIDATED triggers an auto-flip and you may only flip a signal with
  inverse information. `ensemble_scorer.edge_weight` moved off the flat 0.524
  onto a per-market bar at the same time, since `_tier_for`'s own docstring
  warns that fixing one without the other just moves the defect.
- **close_spread carries two opposite conventions.** NFL stores home
  *margin* (corr **+0.598** with actual margin), while NCAAF/NCAAB/NBA/NHL
  store the home *line* (corr -0.771/-0.755/-0.560/-0.233). `grade_side`
  assumed the line convention, so **every NFL spread signal was graded with
  the sign inverted** - and not as a constant bias: for a home favourite it
  marked a cover whenever home lost by less than the line, for a home dog it
  demanded home WIN by the line. `nfl_home_spread_edge` 15.8% -> **37.5%**,
  `nfl_away_spread_edge_v3` 17.8% -> **51.1%**, both matching an independent
  replay. Four NFL signals were wrongly ANTI_VALIDATED (11 -> 7) and two were
  about to be auto-flipped into ~84% ATS signals at full weight.
- Andy's sign question answered: the **signals** are fine. All five NFL
  spread columns agree on one convention, so each takes the side its logic
  implies. The **grader** was inverted. Separately, the close predicts margin
  far better than our models (corr +0.598 vs v3 +0.380 / v4 +0.383; mean
  absolute error 8.50 pts vs 10.15 / 11.11), which is why betting their
  disagreement loses - consistent with the 10-29 decision that disabled the

## 2 · The step that fails most often recorded nothing — again

17 `step_failed` rows in 14 hours. **13 of them are one step**, and
every one carried an empty `stderr_tail`.

Not the hole I fixed yesterday. `daily_card.yml` ran the watchdog bare
and then recorded a critical by calling:

```
run_step.sh --label "consistency_watchdog.py CRITICAL (exit 2)" false
```

**`false` writes nothing**, so there was nothing to capture BY
CONSTRUCTION — duration 0s, rc 1, tail `''`. The watchdog's own output,
the part that names the tripped check and the games, went to the step
log and nowhere queryable. Which is the whole reason Andy's only view of
a critical was the failure email.

I fixed the *branch* in `run_step.sh` yesterday and missed the *call
site*. Same lesson as `feedback_fix_every_writer_to_the_table`.

**Fix:** the step now runs through `run_step.sh --detector`, which
already implements the watchdog's own exit contract
(`consistency_watchdog.py:521` — 0 clean / 1 warnings / 2 critical)
exactly: exit 1 is recorded as a finding and leaves the run green, exit
≥2 fails the run through the gate **and carries the captured output as
the tail**. `run_step.sh` exits 0 on a recorded failure, so later steps
in the job still run — the behaviour the hand-rolled block was protecting
is preserved.

`mlb_pipeline.yml` keeps its bare copy, now with a note saying why:
the watchdog is sport-agnostic, so the gated copy in `daily_card.yml`
already covers every sport, and routing both would write a second
heartbeat row and a second email for the same critical. Log-only there
is the choice, not an oversight.

### And an empty tail now says which kind of empty it is

The non-detector branch captures **stderr only**, on purpose, so the
other ~100 steps keep line buffering. A script that prints its fatal to
stdout therefore records `tail=''` and reads exactly like the telemetry
bug fixed on 11-16. That cost a round this morning on
`fill_ctx_close_from_line_history` (21s, rc=1, nothing to go on). The

## 7.2 B144's last row was a juice-cap swap — and the real bug was next to it

The one MARKET mismatch on 11-16 was `UTEP ML` (badge) against

### 165 live receipts grade a different bet than the read they cite

`public_receipts` upserts `game_read` rows on
`(sport, surface, game_date, source_id)`, `source_id` is the **read's**
id — which does not change when the read's call changes — and
`pick_side/pick_line/pick_label` are frozen by the 20260918b trigger. So
a regenerated read makes the upsert try to UPDATE a frozen row, be
**silently refused** (PATCH returns 200, nothing changes), and the
receipt keeps the first pass's pick forever.

The 10-22 "game day or earlier" fix killed 121 of the 156 then known and

|---|---|
| diverge on side OR line | **165** |

| record on those rows | **95W-65L** |
| record the reads call for | **84W-66L** |
| net | **−11W +1L** |

The worst shape: `Montana @ Kansas St.` — receipt **Under 155.5 → WIN**,
read **Over 134.5 → Loss**, same game, same source row, close 153.5.

`receipt_divergence.py` splits the repair **by consequence**:

- **UNGRADED** diverged receipts move no published number, so

## 7.5 The panel total: de-biasing does not rescue it

First, the blocker nobody had hit: **`ncaab_game_results` and
`ncaab_game_context` share ZERO game_ids** — 841 slug-form ids
(`ncaab_20261105_Northern_Kentucky_South_Dakota`) against 667 hash-form.
So the panel projection could not be joined to a final score at all,

`(date, home, away)`: 421 of 504 matched.

| | median | mean | sd |
|---|---|---|---|
| panel − close | **+6.23** | +5.59 | 5.51 |
| actual − close | −2.50 | −1.25 | 16.35 |

The panel is 6+ points high, and the market's close is itself slightly
high. The rule's record:

| rule | thr 2 | thr 4 | thr 6 |
|---|---|---|---|
| RAW | 46.0% (n=365) | 47.6% (n=290) | 48.3% (n=230) |
| DE-BIASED −6.23 | 54.0% (n=298) | 52.5% (n=183) | 49.5% (n=107) |

Breakeven is 52.4%. The raw rule **loses at every threshold**. De-biased

**0.55 of a standard error**, and the edge *decays* as the threshold
tightens, 54.0 → 52.5 → 49.5. A real signal gets stronger when you demand
more of it. This gets weaker, which is residual bias and noise.

**Verdict: it stays retired, now on a measurement rather than on the
offset alone.** And `audit_projection_calibration` already reports the
sibling: ctx's own `projected_total` runs **4.60 points LOW** (n=499, CI

## 7.6 THE SHARP-MONEY LENS IS OFF IN NCAAB, FOR A REASON THAT ISN'T TRUE

Found while chasing §7.5. `backtest_ncaab_signals` reports three
expression errors — `_HANDLER_EXTERNAL`, `_HANDLER_LINE_FLAG`,
`_HANDLER_SCENARIO` — under the heading "a swallowed error is a FALSE
condition". I nearly logged 19 dead signals on it. **They are not dead.**

`condition_expr = '_HANDLER_LINE_FLAG'` is a **marker**. `ensemble_scorer`
dispatches those signals by `signal_key` — the
`elif base_key == 'sharp_split_confirmed':` branch at
`ensemble_scorer:1382` — and never evaluates the string. 19 signals
across all six sports carry a sentinel, and they are the **sharp-money
and external-handicapper lenses**.

But `set_ncaab_signal_baseline.py` drew exactly the wrong conclusion on
2026-11-03, and it is live:

```
sharp_split_confirmed_ncaab         w=0.0  REJECTED
sharp_split_triple_confirmed_ncaab  w=0.0  REJECTED
sharp_scenario_match_ncaab          w=0.0  REJECTED
  "condition raises NameError (bare _HANDLER_ placeholder) so it can
   never be true on any row"
```

That sentence is false, and the consequence is that **the sharp-money
lens has been switched off in NCAAB — the biggest slate in the book, 53
games tonight — for two weeks.**

They are **not** re-weighted here. "Not measurable by this script" is not
evidence of an edge, and an unmeasured signal must not outweigh a
measured one. The tier is now `NOT_EVALUABLE_HERE` with the honest
reason, weight unchanged at 0.0, so **no pick composition changed** — the
registry just stops asserting something untrue. The backtest labels them
"key-dispatched, not measurable here" instead of listing them as errors.

**What Andy needs to decide, with a measurement first:** these three want
a backtest that drives the HANDLER, not the expression. That is the work
that would let the sharp lens back on in NCAAB with a number behind it.

**B217 — NBA spread reads carry moneyline prices.**
14 of 27 NBA `call_market='rl'` rows are priced outside any real spread band:

    Boston Celtics  -14.5 @ -750     Philadelphia 76ers -12.5 @ -669
    San Antonio     -12.5 @ -625     Cleveland          -11.5 @ -500

laying 4.5 is the *dog's* price on the favourite's side. Every ROI computed
off NBA spread rows is fiction until this is fixed.

## Retracted during this audit

My first pass graded both stores against final scores and reported each ~1.8%
wrong vs reality. **That was my bug, not theirs.** `pick_line` is a magnitude
in 174 rl receipts and signed in 258 — one column, two conventions — so no
single formula grades it (`feedback_magnitude_cannot_express_a_side`). Both
stores returned the *identical* 25 rows, which is the fingerprint of an error
in the measuring code rather than in two independent stores. Dropped; the
line-convention split is itself worth fixing.

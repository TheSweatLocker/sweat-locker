## Signals: the design flaw was real, and it is now fixed

Andy: *"Having no way to copmute those signals accuracy from whatw e store is
a deigin flaw"* and *"why retire automatically intesad of looking liek whats
driving those negative ROI"*.

Both correct, and the second stopped me from shipping a bad call. **931 of
2,034 `signal_attribution` rows recorded no direction** — every `COHORT_*` row
and every `ANCHOR` row carried `signal_side='NEUTRAL'`. So the only number
available for them was *our pick's* result on games where they fired, which is
a measurement of our selection, not of the signal. ANCHOR's "46.2% n=145" and
`HEAVY_HOME_DOG`'s "40.6% n=64" were exactly that, and I used them to
recommend retiring both.

Fixed at the writer (`signal_attribution_snapshot.py`) and **backfilled 863
historical rows** (`backfill_signal_sides.py`) — the cohort tag names its
subject, and ANCHOR's subject is the side the close favours. Recording the
subject is deliberately *not* recording a recommendation: follow-or-fade is now
a calibration output rather than an assumption baked into the writer.

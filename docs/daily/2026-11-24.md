### Why `edge_weight` cannot see it

The bar is `max(side_baseline, flat market breakeven)` — never the price the
signal actually fires at. Confirmed by calling it:

```
side_baseline('NCAAB','ml','HOME_ML') = 0.666   (= registry baseline_pct 66.6)
market_breakeven('ml')                = 0.5833
bar used                              = max(0.666, 0.5833) = 0.666
ncaab_home_efficiency_edge: hr 97.73, n=44  ->  weight 0.8202
```

97.73% against a 66.6% bar is +31pp, which saturates the edge curve. But the
games it fires on are heavy home favourites priced near -1250, where
**breakeven is ~92.6%** — and the refit measured the realised return at
**-6.89%**. The signal clears both bars the scorer knows about and loses money
against the one it does not.

The side baseline barely helps here: with it, 0.8202; without it, 0.8237.
Once a rate is 30pp over the bar the curve is already saturated, so the
2026-10-28 side-baseline fix cannot reach this class of signal.

## 5. Two things I checked and did NOT find a defect in

Recording these so they are not re-opened.

1. **The tier rule is already correct and sophisticated.** I was about to
   "fix" `ncaab_away_shooting_edge` (28.9%, n=197, tagged UNVALIDATED) to
   ANTI_VALIDATED so the auto-fade would flip it. Wrong: `_tier_for` in
   `refit_signal_registry.py:95` **demotes against the side baseline and
   promotes against the price** (Andy's 2026-11-16 change). Blind away-ML in
   NCAAB is ~33.4%, so 28.9% is not inverse information and must not be
   flipped — the module comment says exactly that. Had I shipped it I would
   have inverted a correct family.
   `feedback_a_convention_with_correct_readers`.
2. **Prop signals are NOT judged against the wrong breakeven.** Yesterday I
   listed this as a defect because `market_scope='*'` yields no market. The
   scorer never uses `market_scope` for the bar: `_score_market` filters
   opinions to `CANDIDATES_BY_MARKET[market]` and passes
   `breakeven=market_breakeven(market)` for the market being scored. My census
   passed `None`. **Withdrawn — my artifact, not a defect.**

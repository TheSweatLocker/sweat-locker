   `ensemble_scorer.MAX_TIER_BY_SPORT_MARKET = {('NCAAB','ml'): 'COVERAGE'}`
   caps every NCAAB moneyline to COVERAGE, and the auditor's contradiction

**In fairness to the gate:** the tier exclusion is deliberate and documented
(`feedback_a_detector_must_know_the_intentional_case`) -- COVERAGE reads are

selling it as a play. A COVERAGE contradiction sells nothing. But the prose
still renders in game detail, so the user reads it, and for NCAAB the
exclusion removes 88% of the sport's biggest market from any check at all.

The fix is narrow and matches the file's own stated standard (PRECISION OVER
RECALL, structured claims only): a closing sentence naming `<over|under>
<number>` is as unambiguous as the projected-score rule it already trusts, so
it can be checked at **every** tier without crying wolf. Judging whether a
paragraph *argues a side* still needs a model, and that is correctly out of
scope.

## 14. KenPom: measured, and it reverses my own recommendation

Andy: *"we have so much data through kenpom i want to make sure we are
capitalizing on all of it."*

Yesterday I reported that turnover rate and free throw rate are populated and
read by zero signals, and recommended building signals on both. **Measured,
that recommendation was wrong on FTR and wrong on offensive rebounding.**

### What reads what (NCAAB `signal_sources`, 67 rows, 62 enabled)

| factor | columns | signals reading it |
|---|---|---|
| eFG% | `home/away_efg_o`, `home/away_efg_d` | 2 |
| **Turnover %** | `home/away_to_o` | **0** |
| **Free throw rate** | `home/away_ftr_o` | **0** |
| Off. rebound % | `away_or_o` only | 1 (`ncaab_undersized_home_or_fade`) |
| **adj_em LEVELS** | `home/away_adj_em` | **0** (only the derived gap) |

### Method

Joined `ncaab_game_context` (features) to `ncaab_game_results` (outcomes) --
**two key spaces**: context keys on the Odds-API hex, results on a composed
slug `ncaab_<date>_<away>_<home>`, so the join is on date + normalised team
names, 852 of 1,060 played rows (80.4% on the exact key). 814 have a graded
spread.

Every candidate measured three ways: **ML hit rate against a direction-only
blind control**, **ATS against the same**, and **ROI at the real closing
price**. Blind baselines on the joined set: home ML 73.78%, home ATS 49.39%,
away ATS 50.61%, over 49.51%.

### Result: one candidate, and it is not shippable yet

**Away turnover-rate edge** (`home_to_o - away_to_o >= 3pp`, i.e. the visitor
takes care of the ball better):

| threshold | n | away ATS | vs blind | SE | ROI @ -110 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| >= 1pp | 219 | 55.25% | +4.64pp | 1.37 | +5.5% |
| >= 3pp | 143 | 56.64% | +6.03pp | **1.44** | +8.1% |
| >= 5pp | 92 | 59.78% | +9.17pp | **1.76** | +14.1% |
| >= 8pp | 31 | 48.39% | -2.23pp | -0.25 | -7.6% |

Positive at **every** threshold from 1pp to 6pp, so it is not a single-point
fit. It survives a strength control -- split inside `adj_em_gap` buckets it is
+6.27pp pooled (143 vs 536), so it is not just "the better team". And the HOME
side of the same rule is flat (-0.80pp on n=247), so the effect is asymmetric,
which is interesting given the library fires home-named signals 186 times to
30 away-named.

**But it does not clear the bar, and the moneyline version is a mirage.** The
+26.2% ML ROI I first measured (+37.71u on n=144) is **entirely three dogs**:

```
biggest single win  +16.00u      top 3 = +37.60u of +37.71u  (100%)
drop the 3 best  -> +0.11u, ROI  +0.1%
drop the 5 best  -> -11.14u, ROI -8.0%
```

Same trap as the dog gate I declined earlier today. The ATS figure is the one
worth anything, because every spread bet pays about one unit and cannot be
manufactured by a payout. At **1.44 SE on a single month** it is suggestive,
not established.

**Verdict: shadow it, do not enable it.** Enabling an unmeasured signal is
exactly the defect I found yesterday -- `RAMP_UP_PRIOR` hands 0.50 to anything
without a measurement. Revisit at n >= 400, about mid-January.

### Result: two candidates I should NOT have recommended

**Free throw rate: no edge, either direction.** -0.1pp to -4.1pp, every
threshold, all within 1.3 SE, n=154..300. There is nothing to capitalise on.

**Offensive rebounding: consistently NEGATIVE on the side holding the edge.**
Home -4.5pp (n=283), -5.6pp (n=233), -6.1pp (n=185); away -3.8 to -5.7pp. If
anything an OR edge is a FADE -- which is precisely what the one existing OR
signal, `ncaab_undersized_home_or_fade`, already does.

**adj_em LEVELS are the favourite trap again.** `home_adj_em >= 20` hits
**89.2% on the moneyline against a 73.8% blind control (+15.5pp)** and returns
**-8.8% ROI** at an implied 89.1%. The engine's bar is `max(side_baseline,
flat breakeven)`, so it would score this near saturation while it loses money.
Independent confirmation of
[[feedback_the_bar_is_never_the_price_the_signal_fires_at]] from a completely
different direction. **Do not build adj_em level signals.**

### A measurement error of my own, caught before reporting

My first pass applied one sentinel cap (`>= 40`) to every four-factor column.
A turnover rate cannot reach 40, so that was right for `to_o` -- but FTR's
median is 37.7 and eFG%'s is 52, so the same cap threw away every normal row
and measured FTR and OR **on a biased low-value subset**. It reported
`ftr_edge_away_8pp` at ATS 60.4% (n=48) and eFG% as "0.6% usable". With
per-factor caps, FTR is flat-to-negative on n=184. The `to_o` headline is
unaffected, because real `to_o` maxes at 30.40 so a 40 cut removed only
placeholders.

### The data defect: 166 rows froze a preseason placeholder

| factor | usable both sides | NULL | placeholder |
|---|---|---|---|
| turnover rate | 823/1105 (74.5%) | 116 | **166** |
| eFG% defence | 823/1105 (74.5%) | 116 | **166** |
| FTR / OR / eFG% offence | 823/1105 (74.5%) | 282 | 0 |

166 context rows carry `to_o = 100.0`, `efg_d = 100.0` or `200.0`, and
`efg_o = NULL`. A turnover rate cannot be 100%. They were last written
**2026-10-30..11-15** -- before the four-factor feed had real numbers -- and
nothing re-derives them. `ncaab_team_stats` itself is **clean in all three
seasons** (`to_o` 10.09..30.40, zero sentinels), and the same teams are
correct in their other context rows. So this is a frozen snapshot, not a bad
feed.

Impact is **coverage, not a wrong pick**: both live eFG signals require
`efg_o is not None`, which is NULL on exactly those rows, so they were already
skipping those games. A sentinel of 100 would make `home_efg_o - away_efg_d`
about -48 and the `>= 5.0` condition can never fire either way.

Correcting yesterday's number: I reported turnover rate as "96.2% filled".
That counted placeholders. **Real usable coverage is 74.5%**, the same as
every other factor.

A re-derive from the now-correct `ncaab_team_stats` is straightforward, but it
would newly ENABLE both eFG signals on ~166 games, and `ncaab_away_shooting_edge`
is measured at 28.9% on n=197. Unblocking a read activates a path no gate has
examined ([[feedback_unblocking_a_read_activates_an_ungated_path]]), so it
needs a decision rather than a backfill.

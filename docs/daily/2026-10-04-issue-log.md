## FIXED

### 1. Sweat Card recap never resolved Ohtani and Kim — both had WON
**Symptom** Ohtani Under 1.5 Hits and Kim Under 0.5 Hits sat "Pending" on the
recap while every other pick on the card graded.
**Root cause** The card published `hits_under` while `mlb_pipeline_props` only
ever held the `hits_OVER` row, and the recap overlay demanded an exact
`prop_type` match. Hagen Smith and Robbie Ray resolved purely because their
exact rows happened to exist.
**Fix** `generate_sweat_card` registers the COMPLEMENT: at one line the two
directions are exact opposites, so over 1.5 LOST ⇒ under 1.5 WON. Never
overwrites a real row with a derived one. `898a53d9`
**Result** Recap reads 5-2, 7 of 7 resolved, zero pending.

### 2. Sweat Card prop receipts were ungradeable by design
**Symptom** The same two props stranded in `public_receipts` too.
**Root cause** `public_receipt.sweat_card_rows` wrote `player_name`,
`prop_type`, `pick_line`, `pick_side` all NULL, packing everything into
`source_id` as `prop:Shohei Ohtani|hits_under|1.5`. The grader matches on those
columns. `prop_jerry` receipts populate them and graded fine — same table, same
day, different writer.
**Fix** Writer parses `source_key` into the columns, AND
`grade_public_receipts._grade_sweat_card_props` parses the packed `source_id`
directly — because the writer fix only reaches NEW receipts. `128970b2`

### 3. The Sharp's record was wrong twice over
**Symptom** "only see yesterday record as 3-2 when there was like 17 plays."
**Root cause A** `agg_sharp_card` re-graded `jerry_cache.sharp_card_{date}`,
which is REGENERATED. By the time it ran that cache held 18 items and no NHL,
while the receipts written when the card shipped held 23. An immutable record
derived from a mutable one.
**Root cause B** `app/index.tsx` read the headline with a hardcoded
`.eq('sport','MLB')` — the MLB slice rendered as the whole card.
**Fix** Record now counted from `public_receipts` and graded nowhere; ALL row
emitted every day; app reads ALL for both yesterday and MTD. `898a53d9`,
`8101ecb8`
**Result** 10-03 = 14-9 on 23 picks (was 10-8 on 18, displayed as 3-2).

### 4. POTD read "BUF RL -7" on an NFL card
**Root cause** `jerry_anchor_potd.py:865` hardcoded `f"{team} RL{line_str}"`.
Three lines below it, `sport` had itself been hardcoded 'MLB' until 09-09 —
same function, same family. `jerry_reads.call_text` was already clean.
**Fix** Uses `ensemble_scorer._market_label` (MLB RL / NHL PL / football ATS),
imported not reimplemented — a second copy drifting is how the 09-26 fix for
this same complaint ended up inert. `6ba62cc4`

### 5. The NFL POTD was filed as sport=MLB
**Root cause** The POTD top_8 entry carried no `sport` key and hit a blanket
`_p['sport'] = 'MLB'` default, though both the cache row and its payload say
NFL. **Fix** Threads the real sport, and the default now PRINTS when it fires.
`6ba62cc4`

### 6. Overnight grader going red on a stats report
**Root cause** `reconcile_ncaaf_stat_sources` exits 1 on FAIL and runs inside
`mlb_grade_overnight`. It was RIGHT — `ncaaf_team_stats` was a week stale for
105 teams because the aggregate refreshed Sat 16:13 UTC, before Saturday's
games finished, and NCAAF's Sunday 13:00 run had not fired since 09-27. But it
also named the wrong table ("the log is short a game" when the AGGREGATE was
short). **Fix** `--report-only` for the grader host, correct side named,
aggregate refreshed → now reports OK. `77be9729`, `f6cb033a`

### 7. External handicapper picks were 100% inverted and sport-blind
See `project_external_handler_sport_blind_1003`. `0d018b03`

## OPEN — root cause known, fix proposed, NOT shipped

### A. Box-score settle cannot fire at all
**Found** Every receipt the inherit misses dies at `boxscore:no_boxscore`.
`settle_prop_receipts` calls the live MLB gameLog API, which answers nothing
for these dates — Ohtani returns None for 10-03 though he played and finished
on 1. The fallback designed to survive a pruned source can itself never fire.
**Proposed** Settle from `mlb_pipeline_props.final_value` — our own ingested
box score, 404 of 407 rows on 10-03 across 88 players. Written and working
(`8101ecb8`) but **NOT wired in**: `--validate` over 14 days gives 1078/1201
agreement with **123 disagreements**, concentrated in FADE families where
`pick_side` is the prop's side rather than the side we backed.
**Next** Explain those 123 before applying. 89.8% is not good enough to write
to published receipts.

### B. Receipt claim columns are immutable — and that hid a false success
`public_receipts` accepts grading columns and silently refuses claim columns
(`player_name`, `prop_type`, `pick_line`, `pick_side`, `pick_label`): a PATCH
returns 200 with the row unchanged. The policy is CORRECT. But
`grade_stranded_receipts` reported "5 graded, each verified by read-back" when
only the grades landed — the check stopped at "a row came back". Fixed to
verify the value. A blocked update returning 200 is the same shape as the
204-is-not-a-write trap and is worth a wider audit.

### C. Skubal receipt: label and source disagree
`"Tarik Skubal Over 7.5 Ks"` points at `source_id ks_over|6.5`; he threw 7 —
WIN at one line, LOSS at the other. Its `matchup` ALSO says "Atlanta Braves @
Los Angeles Dodgers" (he is Detroit), so that receipt has two independent
defects. Both graders now refuse it and print why rather than silently picking
a side. **Needs a human decision on which line was published.**

### D. BUF -7 missing from today's Sharp — EXPLAINED, and partly self-inflicted
**Symptom** Today's Sharp has MLB 2 + NFL 1 (PHI +3). BUF -7 — STRONG/70,
anchor weight 0.0, today's POTD — is absent.
**Cause** The card HARD-LOCKS at 12:00 ET and refuses republish even with
`--force` (`SHARP_CARD_EMERGENCY_UNLOCK=1` to override). The stored card is the
11:55 build, where NFL composed only PHI. BUF composes NOW because **I
recomposed the NFL context at ~14:00** with `NFL_UNLOCK_WEEK=1`.
**So the lock is working correctly and I created the mismatch**: the engine's
current view and the locked card legitimately disagree because I changed the
inputs after the card was sealed.
**Lesson** Do not recompose a published surface's inputs after its lock. If a
recompose is genuinely needed post-lock, the card has to be re-locked
deliberately and the change disclosed — not left as a silent divergence
between what shipped and what the engine now says.
**Open question for Andy** Today's Sharp ships 3 items while yesterday shipped
23. The thin slate is real (MLB ctx=2, NCAAF=0 on a Sunday), but whether a
3-item Sharp should publish at all is a product call.

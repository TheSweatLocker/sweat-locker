### 2. The Sweat Card receipt had no sync path at all

This is the part worth fixing. Even after the read graded Win *and*
`refresh_card_grades` wrote Win into the card blob, the receipt stayed NULL.

`sync_receipt_grades.SOURCES` covered three:

|---|---|---|
| `jerry_reads` · `ledger_snapshots` · `prop_jerry_reads` | 0 | yes |
| `jerry_cache.sweat_card` | 1 | **no** ← the Lakers |

caller, syncing one source. `jerry_reads`, `prop_jerry_reads` and the card
surfaces were never synced on a schedule at all. Now `--source all`.

## Three graders where there were none

**`jerry_cache.sweat_card`** → new blob pass in `sync_receipt_grades`. One
trap: the blob holds the same pick in **two lists** — `football_picks` had the
Lakers as `Win`, `unified_top_picks` had no result. `refresh_card_grades`
writes the first. Reading the wrong one would have looked like the fix failed.

**`jerry_cache.sharp_card`** → deliberately **not** added. Its blob is
`items[]` with conviction, line, odds, pick, tier and **no result field at
all**. Listing it would examine 300+ receipts and grade none, which looks
like a working sync.

**`line_snapshot` (fade) and any game-market receipt** → new
`grade_game_market_receipts.py`, which grades from the final score with no
source row needed. `generate_the_fade` captures receipts with a comment
saying *"so the record grades itself the way every other surface does"* and
grades nothing; `reconcile_receipt_results` skips any group with
`len(rows) < 2`, and the Fade's whole point is a side nobody else took, so it
is always a group of one. 7 of 10 fade receipts were graded only because
another surface happened to publish the same bet.

## The spread sign convention, settled

The first cut of that grader refused spreads, citing
`project_close_spread_sign_bug_914` (convention inverted between NFL and
NCAAF). Right default, wrong resting place — it left the Lakers Sharp Card
receipt with no path to ever grade.

Settled it from data: all **344** graded spread receipts carrying
`pick_side` + `pick_line` + `game_id`, recomputed from the final score under
both readings and compared to the stored grade.

| sport | line signed **for the side** | line as market number |
|---|---|---|
| NCAAF | **97.2%** (239/246) | 85.0% |
| NHL | **100%** (5/5) | 80.0% |
| NBA | 100% (8/8) | 100% — all 8 are HOME picks, where the readings **coincide**, so NBA does not discriminate |
| MLB | 66.7% (14/21) | 57.1% |

`pick_line` is already signed from the picked side's point of view. NCAAF
decides it at n=246.

**MLB excluded.** 66.7% on n=21 is noise with a lean, not a convention, and
its spread market is the run line, which has its own quirks. MLB spread
receipts stay ungraded here rather than graded two times in three.

Result: `Los Angeles Lakers -5.5 → WIN (127-105, HOME margin +22 with -5.5 =
+16.5)`.

## Follow-up: the POTD sync, and a guard I had left out

Fixing the 400 made 10 stranded POTD receipts gradeable — two from 10-21 and
**eight stale ones back to July** that the broken SELECT had been hiding.
Verified every one against its source's `lean` before trusting the write:
**all same-bet, 0 wrong grades**.

But the conflict count went 503 → 512, and those nine were mine to explain.
They were not new corruption — they became *visible* once the POTD source
could be read. Classifying all 267 POTD-sourced receipts against the
calendar:

| | n |
|---|---|
| agree | **244** |
| **drift** — receipt and source name *different bets* | 20 |
| **real conflict** — same bet, different grade | **3** |

The drift is structural, not a defect: the POTD gets **replaced during the
day** (that is what the `best_bet_<date>_<sport>` archive rows are for), and
the receipt keeps the original pick while `daily_best_bet_history` moves on.

```
10-15  receipt 'Cleveland Guardians ML'       = WIN
       source  'Los Angeles Dodgers ML'       = LOSS
10-05  receipt 'No Play (discipline pass)'    = NO_ACTION
       source  'Freddy Peralta Over 13.5 Outs'= WIN
```

Neither grade is wrong. They are not the same bet.

**My pass had no identity check.** `sync_source` has `ident()` for exactly
this, and its comment says why — *"The source row no longer describes this
bet, so its grade says nothing about this receipt in either direction."* I
wrote the new pass without one, so two thirds of what it called a conflict
was noise, and the three that matter would have been buried in it. Added
`_same_bet()`, which strips the decorative parenthetical each surface appends
(`(Jerry 85/100)`, `(resolver STRONG)`).

# 2026-10-13 — data-feed fixes, continued

Continues the cross-sport audit from `docs/daily/2026-10-12.md`.

## FIXED · one absent column was dropping all 17 trend fields (MLB + NBA)

`enrich_team_trends` PATCHes 17 season-trend fields onto `<sport>_game_context`.
`mlb_game_context` and `nba_game_context` have no `*_season_ats_pushes` or
`*_season_ou_pushes` (NFL and NCAAF do), and **PostgREST rejects a PATCH naming
any absent column as a whole** — 400 PGRST204, all 17 fields discarded. So MLB
wrote nothing on every run while the upstream source was correct and current
(`team_season_trends` holds all 30 MLB teams for 2026, refreshed daily).

That is the root cause of 12 enabled MLB signals evaluating NULL:
`home/away_team_ats_hot_season`, `_ats_cold_season`,
`home/away_team_over_trend_season`, `_under_trend_season`,
`both_teams_over_trend_season`, `both_teams_under_trend_season`.

**`--dry-run` never issues the PATCH**, so the failure was invisible in exactly
the mode used to verify the job: dry-run printed `H=64.3% A=61.3%`, the live run
printed `0/2 patched`.

Fixed with a PGRST204 strip-retry (mirroring `nfl_game_context.upsert_context`)
that also prints which columns it stripped. MLB now 2/2 patched,
`home_season_cover_pct` = 64.3 / 61.2 where it was NULL. Migration
`20261013a_mlb_nba_season_push_columns.sql` adds the four columns; the
strip-retry makes it non-blocking.

## FIXED · NBA externals were empty for TIMING, not parsing

`external_picks` held 0 NBA rows while all 6 sources reported "ok". On the
**same 25 candidate URLs**, a 07:02 ET pull returned 0 picks and a 12:04 ET
pull returned 12. OddsCrowd publishes its Bets%/Money% block only as a game
approaches, and `externals_oddscrowd` requires that block to emit a pick at
all.

Ruled out first rather than assumed: the title regex, the date window, the
"Odds Comparison" anchor and all three market patterns match on a live NBA
page, and `find_game_id` resolves Cleveland@Orlando, NYK@Toronto and
GSW@Lakers against the real slate. Every gate passed; only the clock was wrong.

**The cron slot was chosen from measured lateness.** These crons do not fire
when they say. From `workflow_heartbeat`, 294 starts:

| nominal UTC | median late | p90 |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | +4.5h | +6.5h |
| 15 | +4.3–4.8h | +6.7h |
| 18 | **+2.9h** | +4.6h |
| 20 | +2.8h | +4.1h |
| 22 | +2.1h | +3.4h |

My first pass used `22 UTC` on nominal reasoning ("6pm EDT, before a 7pm tip").
That actually lands **~8:10pm ET — after the tips**. `18 UTC` lands ~4:54pm ET
median, ~6:33pm ET at p90: both ahead of the earliest tip and late enough for
splits. Added as a second NBA cron.

Result: **NBA externals 0 → 28** (scoresandodds 16, oddscrowd 12, across
ml/rl/total). And NHL — whose `15:20` slot already lands ~4pm ET by the same
arithmetic — produced **146 picks from 6/6 sources** on an afternoon pull, 133
landing today, more than any prior single day, taking NHL from ~329 to 462
lifetime.

### Logged for decision: the 3 star-out signals still cannot fire

The data now exists, but the signals read a column name that does not:

| signal | reads | should read |
|---|---|---|
| `nba_home_star_out_fade` | `ctx.home_star_out` (boolean) | `ctx.home_injury_impact` or `ctx.home_starters_out` |
| `nba_away_star_out_fade` | `ctx.away_star_out` | same |
| `nba_prop_star_out_teammate_boost` | both | same |

**Not changed, deliberately.** Two reasons:

1. It is a pick-composition change, which CLAUDE.md says to ask about.
2. "Star out" is not the same as "a starter is out", and the honest version
   needs a threshold — e.g. `float(ctx.home_injury_impact) >= 15` (roughly a
   starting-calibre scorer's ppg; today's slate would fire on GSW 31.7, LAC
   44.7, POR 19.4 but not MIN 11.8 or IND 5.4). **There is no NBA graded
   history to calibrate that bar against** — the season opens 10-20 — so
   shipping it would put an uncalibrated threshold straight into live picks.

Recommended: wire the condition to `injury_impact >= 15` and run it through the
`rule_registry` shadow for the first two weeks of the season, promoting on
evidence rather than on my guess.

## FIXED · park factors were a coin flip between two vintages

Chasing the `park_factor` threshold turned up something larger.
`mlb_park_factors` holds **61 rows for 30 teams** — every club has both a
2026-03-23 and a 2026-04-14 row — and **28 of 30 CONFLICT**:

| team | one vintage | the other |
|---|---|---|
| Texas Rangers | 80 | 103 |
| Oakland | 94 | 113 |
| LA Dodgers | 93 | 111 |
| Colorado | 118 | 133 (3 rows) |

`game_context.get_park_factors()` did `data[0]` with **no `order`**, and
PostgREST does not guarantee row order without one — so a 23-point swing in
`run_factor` was decided arbitrarily, per request, for every MLB game. Same
failure class as `project_madden_week_collision_1001`: two valid-looking rows,
no tiebreak, consumer silently picks one.

Fixed to `order=updated_at.desc&limit=1`. Verified deterministic and stable
across repeated calls.

### Logged for root cause: the park data itself is wrong

Determinism is not correctness. **Both vintages are from March/April 2026 and
it is now October** — ~6 months stale, computed on early-season samples. Worse,
the *newest* vintage carries the implausible values: Texas 80 (Globe Life Field
is a fairly neutral retractable-roof park) and Coors 133. The older 03-23 row
had Texas at 103.

Park factors need recomputing on full-season data, and the table needs a unique
constraint on (team, handedness) so vintages replace rather than accumulate.

### `park_factor` signals: corrected, left disabled

`pitcher_prop_park_hitter` / `pitcher_prop_park_pitcher` read `ctx.park_factor`
— a column that never existed. Rewired to `ctx.park_run_factor` and the
threshold rescaled from the ratio the author intended (`1.03` / `0.97`) to the
100-centred scale the column uses (`103` / `97`). As written the hitter
condition was `97 >= 1.03`, **true on every game**.

Firing rate on 36 context rows: `>=103` → 5, `<=97` → 24, against 100% under
the bug. League-wide (`mlb_park_factors`, median 99, mean 100.2) the rescaled
bars split 33% / 44%, which is what a hitter-park / pitcher-park classifier
should look like.

**Held `enabled=false`** rather than shipped: wiring a signal to 6-month-old
data carrying implausible extremes would be fixing the symptom and keeping the
defect. Enable once park factors are refreshed.

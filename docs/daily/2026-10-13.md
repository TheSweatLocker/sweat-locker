## FIXED · one absent column was dropping all 17 trend fields (MLB + NBA)

`enrich_team_trends` PATCHes 17 season-trend fields onto `<sport>_game_context`.
`mlb_game_context` and `nba_game_context` have no `*_season_ats_pushes` or
`*_season_ou_pushes` (NFL and NCAAF do), and **PostgREST rejects a PATCH naming
any absent column as a whole** — 400 PGRST204, all 17 fields discarded. So MLB
wrote nothing on every run while the upstream source was correct and current
(`team_season_trends` holds all 30 MLB teams for 2026, refreshed daily).

That is the root cause of 12 enabled MLB signals evaluating NULL:
`home/away_team_ats_hot_season`, `_ats_cold_season`,
`home/away_team_over_trend_season`, `_under_trend_season`,
`both_teams_over_trend_season`, `both_teams_under_trend_season`.

**`--dry-run` never issues the PATCH**, so the failure was invisible in exactly
the mode used to verify the job: dry-run printed `H=64.3% A=61.3%`, the live run
printed `0/2 patched`.

Fixed with a PGRST204 strip-retry (mirroring `nfl_game_context.upsert_context`)
that also prints which columns it stripped. MLB now 2/2 patched,
`home_season_cover_pct` = 64.3 / 61.2 where it was NULL. Migration
`20261013a_mlb_nba_season_push_columns.sql` adds the four columns; the
strip-retry makes it non-blocking.

## FIXED · NBA externals were empty for TIMING, not parsing

`external_picks` held 0 NBA rows while all 6 sources reported "ok". On the
**same 25 candidate URLs**, a 07:02 ET pull returned 0 picks and a 12:04 ET
pull returned 12. OddsCrowd publishes its Bets%/Money% block only as a game
approaches, and `externals_oddscrowd` requires that block to emit a pick at

page, and `find_game_id` resolves Cleveland@Orlando, NYK@Toronto and
GSW@Lakers against the real slate. Every gate passed; only the clock was wrong.

**The cron slot was chosen from measured lateness.** These crons do not fire
when they say. From `workflow_heartbeat`, 294 starts:

| nominal UTC | median late | p90 |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | +4.5h | +6.5h |
| 15 | +4.3–4.8h | +6.7h |
| 18 | **+2.9h** | +4.6h |
| 20 | +2.8h | +4.1h |
| 22 | +2.1h | +3.4h |

My first pass used `22 UTC` on nominal reasoning ("6pm EDT, before a 7pm tip").
That actually lands **~8:10pm ET — after the tips**. `18 UTC` lands ~4:54pm ET
median, ~6:33pm ET at p90: both ahead of the earliest tip and late enough for

## FIXED · park factors were a coin flip between two vintages

Chasing the `park_factor` threshold turned up something larger.
`mlb_park_factors` holds **61 rows for 30 teams** — every club has both a
2026-03-23 and a 2026-04-14 row — and **28 of 30 CONFLICT**:

| team | one vintage | the other |
|---|---|---|
| Texas Rangers | 80 | 103 |
| Oakland | 94 | 113 |
| LA Dodgers | 93 | 111 |
| Colorado | 118 | 133 (3 rows) |

`game_context.get_park_factors()` did `data[0]` with **no `order`**, and
PostgREST does not guarantee row order without one — so a 23-point swing in
`run_factor` was decided arbitrarily, per request, for every MLB game. Same
failure class as `project_madden_week_collision_1001`: two valid-looking rows,
no tiebreak, consumer silently picks one.

Fixed to `order=updated_at.desc&limit=1`. Verified deterministic and stable
across repeated calls.

### Logged for root cause: the park data itself is wrong

Determinism is not correctness. **Both vintages are from March/April 2026 and
it is now October** — ~6 months stale, computed on early-season samples. Worse,
the *newest* vintage carries the implausible values: Texas 80 (Globe Life Field
is a fairly neutral retractable-roof park) and Coors 133. The older 03-23 row
had Texas at 103.

Park factors need recomputing on full-season data, and the table needs a unique
constraint on (team, handedness) so vintages replace rather than accumulate.

### `park_factor` signals: corrected, left disabled

`pitcher_prop_park_hitter` / `pitcher_prop_park_pitcher` read `ctx.park_factor`
— a column that never existed. Rewired to `ctx.park_run_factor` and the
threshold rescaled from the ratio the author intended (`1.03` / `0.97`) to the
100-centred scale the column uses (`103` / `97`). As written the hitter
condition was `97 >= 1.03`, **true on every game**.

Firing rate on 36 context rows: `>=103` → 5, `<=97` → 24, against 100% under
the bug. League-wide (`mlb_park_factors`, median 99, mean 100.2) the rescaled
bars split 33% / 44%, which is what a hitter-park / pitcher-park classifier
should look like.

**Held `enabled=false`** rather than shipped: wiring a signal to 6-month-old
data carrying implausible extremes would be fixing the symptom and keeping the
defect. Enable once park factors are refreshed.

## DEEP DIVE · NFL/NCAAF models, data verification (Andy 10-13)

### 1 · v4_spread is a CONSTANT, not a model — and 6 signals read it

`v4_spread` across the 59 rows that carry it:

- **9 distinct values**, sd **0.276**, range 0.78–2.93, mean 1.745
- 27 of 59 games share the identical value `1.73`
- `close_spread` sd for contrast: **6.377** (23x larger)
- `v4_confidence` = 0.311 on **all 59** rows
- `v4_features_used` carries **no team-strength features at all** — only
  `temp`, `wind`, `week`, `is_dome`, `*_bye`, `*_rest`, `div_game`,
  `is_playoff`, `*_short_week`

It cannot distinguish CHI@CAR (line −21.5) from DAL@GB (line 1.5); both get
~1.7. Regressed on the line: **slope +0.005 ± 0.006**. This confirms
`project_nfl_v4_no_team_features_927` — known on 9/27, still wired.

Because v4 is always positive, the signals reading it degenerate. Evaluated
against the 59 live rows:

| signal | fires | what it actually is |
|---|---|---|
| `nfl_model_consensus_home_spread` | **0/59** | **cannot fire** — requires v4 < 0 |
| `nfl_model_consensus_away_spread` | 20/59 | v4 term always true → **panel-only** |
| `nfl_away_spread_edge` | 24/59 | reduces to close_spread >= 3.75 |
| `nfl_home_spread_edge` | 17/59 | reduces to close_spread <= -0.25 |
| `nfl_spread_consensus_away_rl` | 28/59 | v4 vote term is a line test |
| `nfl_spread_consensus_home_rl` | 17/59 | same |
| `v4_spread_nfl` (disabled) | 0/59 | needs abs(v4) >= 3.0; max is 2.93 |

**NFL's model consensus is structurally one-sided — the home side can never
fire.** And the "two-model agreement" is one compressed model plus a constant.

### 2 · Mimicry: LEVELS looked independent, DEVIATIONS are not

My first pass reported v4 vs panel at **r = −0.001** and called them two
independent takes. That measured the wrong thing. The engine's consensus is
about agreeing on a side **relative to the line**, so what matters is the
deviation:

| | correlation (n=55) |
|---|---|
| levels — corr(v4, panel margin) | **+0.076** |
| **deviations — corr(v4−line, panel−line)** | **+0.836** |

Cause: `panel` regressed on the line has **slope +0.434** — compressed to 43%
of market scale (~2.3x under-projection, matching
`project_sp_plus_compression_927`), and v4 is a constant. Both deviations are
therefore dominated by `−line`, which is why they move together.

**Correlate DEVIATIONS, never levels, when asking whether models are
independent.**

### 4 · SOR BEATS THE LINE IN NFL — the one real edge found

Walk-forward (rating fitted only on games strictly prior), scored against the
residual `margin + home_line`:

| NFL bucket | n | slope | 95% CI | topQ−botQ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | 829 | +0.245 | [−0.054, +0.543] | +4.8pp |
| **3-7** | 2061 | **+0.153** | **[+0.027, +0.280]** | +1.9pp |
| 7-14 | 1323 | +0.079 | [−0.022, +0.180] | +0.4pp |
| 14+ | 149 | −0.088 | [−0.318, +0.141] | −13.8pp |
| **ALL** | **4362** | **+0.102** | **[+0.034, +0.171]** | |

Monotone in line size, and it independently reproduces the hypothesis in
migration `20261010a` (+4.39 → +1.94 → +0.73 → +0.23) — same shape, now
measured against the residual with a leak-free fit.

**NCAAF does not replicate**: ALL +0.029 CI[−0.047,+0.104], 0 of 8 buckets
clear, 3-7 is −0.087 against NFL's +0.153. **SOS carries nothing in either
sport.**

Honest magnitude: corr(SOR gap, line margin) = +0.831, so SOR mostly
re-derives the market; only the deviation carries signal, and the quintile
cover spread (+4.8pp / +1.9pp) sits near the 52.38% breakeven. **SOR alone is
not a bet** — its value is being a measured, leak-free input the engine does
not have. **Zero of 837 signal_sources rows reference sor or sos.**

### Not leaked, just young

`nfl_game_context.home_sor` began being written 2026-10-10, so the graded
sample is NFL 14 / NCAAF 45. Checked rather than assumed: every populated NFL
row is October or later and the rows differing from today's team value are
precisely the already-played ones, i.e. they kept their point-in-time figure.
The limit is sample size, not contamination — hence the walk-forward rebuild.

### Priority order out of this

1. **Fix or retire `v4_spread`.** It feeds 6 enabled signals, one of which
   cannot fire and one of which is a no-op term. Either give it team features
   or remove it from the consensus — right now the consensus is panel-only
   while being advertised as two models.
2. **Build a SOR signal for NFL only**, gated to lines under ~7, shadowed
   first. It is the only input measured to move the residual.
3. **De-duplicate the model columns** so a future audit is not misled.
4. Recalibration should wait for 1–3; retuning weights over a constant and a

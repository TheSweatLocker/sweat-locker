### The structural finding: one column, two meanings

`public_splits_archive.pick_side` means the PUBLIC side on oddscrowd rows and
the SHARP side on fr/ftp/cz rows, decided by whichever scraper populated it:

| reader | keyed on | so pick_side is |
|---|---|---|
| `latest_oc` -> line_snapshot | `pick_side` (majority tickets) | the PUBLIC side |
| `latest_fr` | `sharp_side_norm` | the SHARP side |
| `latest_ftp` | `sharp_side_norm` | the SHARP side |

sharp side. This is the mechanical explanation for

other. `grade_money_flow.py` is NOT affected: it reads `line_snapshot` only,

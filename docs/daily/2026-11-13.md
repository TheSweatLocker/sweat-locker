### What conviction was

`ensemble_scorer:3071`:

```
base         = 50 + min(win_score * 10, 30)
margin_boost = min(max(margin, 0) * 20, 15)
conviction   = clamp(base + margin_boost, 45, 97)
```

`win_score` is a weighted vote over `signal_registry`, and every NCAAB signal
sits at 0.000–0.153 by construction (`set_ncaab_signal_baseline`: 2 of 65
PROVISIONAL, rest 0.000 or a 0.020 prior). So `win_score` never leaves
~0.0–0.4 and conviction never leaves 50–54 — **266 of 274 rows (97%), max
62**. The formula was not broken. It was honestly reporting an empty signal
library, and no rescaling could add information it never had.

11-01..11-06, every number below scored on 11-07..11-12.

| ML picks n=156 | 0.1975 | 0.1577 | **0.1543** |
| SPREAD picks n=167 | 0.2553 | 0.2619 | **0.2500** |

The blend weight was **fitted, not chosen** — minimise train Brier:

- ML `w_model = 0.00`. The training half says ignore our model entirely.
- SPREAD `w_model = 0.26`, and that weight **loses** out of sample (0.2510 vs

So our model beats a constant on ML and is beaten by the price; on spreads it
cannot beat a constant at all. Both weights are 0.0 and P(win) **is** the

(n=315) at **ROI −7.1%, CI [−13.2, −1.3]** — 297 of 315 are the favourite, so
the hit rate is the price, not an edge.

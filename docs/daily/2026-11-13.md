### What conviction was

`ensemble_scorer:3071`:

```
base         = 50 + min(win_score * 10, 30)
margin_boost = min(max(margin, 0) * 20, 15)
conviction   = clamp(base + margin_boost, 45, 97)
```

`win_score` is a weighted vote over `signal_registry`, and every NCAAB signal
sits at 0.000–0.153 by construction (`set_ncaab_signal_baseline`: 2 of 65
PROVISIONAL, rest 0.000 or a 0.020 prior). So `win_score` never leaves
~0.0–0.4 and conviction never leaves 50–54 — **266 of 274 rows (97%), max
62**. The formula was not broken. It was honestly reporting an empty signal
library, and no rescaling could add information it never had.

### The measurement

closing lines screened through `posted_line.is_postable`. Constants fitted on
11-01..11-06, every number below scored on 11-07..11-12.

Brier, lower is better:

| | CONSTANT | ours | MARKET de-vigged |
|---|---|---|---|
| ML picks n=156 | 0.1975 | 0.1577 | **0.1543** |
| SPREAD picks n=167 | 0.2553 | 0.2619 | **0.2500** |

The blend weight was **fitted, not chosen** — minimise train Brier:

- ML `w_model = 0.00`. The training half says ignore our model entirely.
- SPREAD `w_model = 0.26`, and that weight **loses** out of sample (0.2510 vs
  0.2500 for the baseline it was meant to beat).

So our model beats a constant on ML and is beaten by the price; on spreads it
cannot beat a constant at all. Both weights are 0.0 and P(win) **is** the
de-vigged price. Its disagreement with the price is not worth betting:
`>=3pp` n=73 ROI −7.2%, `>=6pp` n=46 −5.4%. And MC moneyline picks hit 78.4%
(n=315) at **ROI −7.1%, CI [−13.2, −1.3]** — 297 of 315 are the favourite, so
the hit rate is the price, not an edge.

### The adoption

`conviction` for NCAAB is now `ncaab_conviction`'s calibrated P(our side
wins). Shipped in `9cb65318`. The shadow audit confirms it end to end:
**adoption holds — conviction == _cal_conviction on all 33 picks.**

This is what the field was always *defined* to be. `model_edge.edge_pp` is
literally `conviction - implied_prob*100`, and `conviction_scale.py:175`
already said "conviction stays the probability". The old 50–54 band was a
**fake** probability: it asserted "we think this is a 51% shot" about a
-250 favourite, printed a −20pp edge on every chalk play, and capped the
whole sport to COVERAGE for the wrong reason.

### The floor was inventing edge on every dog

The blocker I found by simulating adoption before shipping it. `CONV_MIN`
was 50 — "a pick we would publish is at worst a coin flip", which is
reasoning about *selection* applied to a *probability*:

```
Pittsburgh ML      +135  implied 42.6  conv 50  ->  edge_pp +7.4
Mississippi St ML  +140  implied 41.7  conv 50  ->  edge_pp +8.3
```

Neither number came from the model; both came from the clamp. Worse, a
POSITIVE edge is the one case `cap_tier_for_edge` leaves alone — so the
floor did not just print a wrong figure, it switched **off** the guard that
demotes an unbacked pick. `CONV_MIN` is 1. Those two now read conviction
41/40 at edge −1.6/−1.7, and the board shows **0 picks with positive edge**:
every priced NCAAB pick sits at −1.6 to −4.3pp, which is the vig, honestly
stated.

### A probability cannot rank quality

The hazard adoption creates, measured on tonight's board rather than
argued:

```
conv 69  -275  Saint Mary's      <- ranks FIRST
conv 68  -238  Virginia          <- wins once -275 fails the juice gate
conv 63  -190  North Carolina
...
conv 41  +135  Pittsburgh        <- ranks LAST
```

POTD ordering becomes **strictly by price**, and the −250 juice gate then
hands it the most juiced survivor — the documented heavy-favourite trap,
arrived at structurally, exactly as the favourite bias became the market
chooser on 10-29. `conviction_scale.PROBABILITY_CONVICTION` now refuses to
rank or POTD-gate such a sport, and POTD prints the reason instead of the
sport going silently absent.

The separator that *should* serve here is `conviction_rank`, and it is
**NULL on 32 of 32** NCAAB picks: `stamp_conviction_rank` requires a
`margin` the NCAAB ensemble never emits, so `_margin_from` returns None and
all 32 skip. Scheduled since 11-08, has never written a row for this sport.
I did **not** fake a margin from the conviction model — its units are
points, and `SIGNAL_SPANS['NCAAB']` margin p90 is 0.09, so a points margin
would saturate every pick at rank 97.

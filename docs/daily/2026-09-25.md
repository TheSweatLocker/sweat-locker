## 🚨 P0 — orphaned prop receipts: ~619 published picks can never be graded

`public_receipts` prop rows inherit their result from `prop_jerry_reads` via
`source_id`. Those source rows are regenerated (delete + reinsert) and come back with
new ids, so the receipts point at ids that no longer exist. The link is broken
permanently.

Measured on 09-23: **240 of 241** ungraded prop receipts have a `source_id` absent
from `prop_jerry_reads`. Orphan ids sit *inside* the live id range for that date
(reads 108836..110133, orphans 108828..109193), which is what a regenerate looks like.

| date | prop_jerry_reads | public_receipts | receipts ungraded |
|---|---|---|---|
| 09-22 | 209 (0 ungraded) | 424 | 215 (51%) |
| 09-23 | 189 (0 ungraded) | 429 | 241 (56%) |
| 09-24 | 145 (0 ungraded) | 307 | 162 (53%) |

The grader itself is fine — `prop_jerry_reads` is 100% graded. Only the receipt copy is
stranded, and `hits_under` is worst hit (519 of the ungraded) because it is the largest
family.

Two things hid this:
1. `grade_public_receipts.py` reports the skip as `source_row_ungraded`, which
   conflates "row is ungraded" with "row does not exist". It is the second.
2. The workflow step is `continue-on-error: true`, so nothing ever went red.

**Recommended fix:** grade prop receipts on a natural key
(`sport, game_date, player_name, prop_type, pick_line, pick_side`) instead of
`source_id`. That survives regeneration. Also split the skip counter so
"not found" is distinguishable from "ungraded". Not applied — needs Andy's call.

Related: [[project_public_receipts_integrity_918]] is the P0 that this belongs to.

### The FADE trap (caught by --validate before any write)

A receipt's `pick_side` is NOT always the side we backed. On a FADE we bet the
opposite side, but the receipt records the *prop's* side. All 72 graded FADE
prop receipts since 09-15 show the prop side while carrying the faded side's
W/L — "Jose Quintana UNDER 10.5" holds the OVER's result.

First validation run: 22/26, with all four disagreements being FADEs. After the
guard: **107/107**, FADEs included.

🟠 **Still open — users are shown the wrong side.** The receipt WRITER records
the prop's side rather than the backed side on FADE receipts. 72 rows since
09-15. Not fixed; it changes what a published receipt says, so it needs a call.

### The pattern behind the corrections

Three load-bearing wrong claims in two days, same shape every time, and
the pipeline data was right in all three:

| claim | reality | fault |
|---|---|---|
| "no NCAAB ML price was ever stored" (`close_home_ml` 0) | `home_ml_close` held 5,319 | wrong column name |
| per-bucket spread cover table on n=5,305 | 4,256 lines were cross-book averages | unvalidated field |
| "14 games tipping 21:00 ET or later tonight" | 3 were the next day | missing date filter |

Three conclusions, three throwaway queries, zero committed scripts. The
app renders from pipeline tables behind gates; the chat claims came from
ad-hoc SQL with nothing checking them. The least-verified output in the
system was being delivered with the most confidence.

Standing rule from here: **any slate or pick list goes through a committed
script that prints `game_date` on every row and refuses a row outside the
requested date.**

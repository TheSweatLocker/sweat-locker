### NCAAB: 11 of 47 games dark, one root cause, three surfaces

No close line, no money flow, and no KenPom ratings on the same 11 games.
Cross-tabbing the three gaps against name resolution settled it immediately:
**11 of 11 dark games carried a team name that resolves to nothing in
`ncaab_team_aliases`, while 34 of 36 lit games resolved.**

The names are the Odds API's own spelling - abbreviated school *with* the mascot,
`San Diego St Aztecs` - a form `build_enriched_alt_names` has never emitted.
Every variant it derives is of the school ALONE (`San Diego St.`,
`San Diego State`) or ESPN's one full displayName (`San Diego State Aztecs`).
The cross-product was the whole gap.

It then split downstream in two ways that look unrelated, which is why this read
as several separate problems: `ncaab_odds_pull`'s alias gate **drops the event**
(no row, no line, no prices), while `ncaab_game_context.canonical_team`
**falls back to identity** and stores the raw odds name, which matches nothing in
`ncaab_team_stats`, so ratings and projections come back NULL.

Fixed at the generator (`44e5372a`) - cross the mascot with the school variants
and the canonical's own variants, since canonical carries forms ESPN's `location`
does not (Albany vs UAlbany, Cal Baptist vs California Baptist, Sam Houston St.
vs Sam Houston). 8 of 8 observed forms now generate. Two irreducible ones named
explicitly: Grand Canyon Antelopes (ESPN mascot is "Lopes") and IUPUI Jaguars
(IU Indianapolis since 2024, canonical `IU Indy`, feed still ships the retired
name).

Applied live rather than waiting on the workflow. Collision-checked first - no
proposed key was claimed by another school, every one passes the first-token test
from `feedback_prefix_strip_can_name_another_school` - then 15 rows patched and
**verified by readback**, then the enricher run over all 362 rows.

Result, measured end to end:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| alias name variants | 1,473 | **1,839** |
| odds events skipped unmapped | 10 | **3** |
| events priced into context | 27 | **32** |
| originally-dark games resolved | 0/11 | **9/11** |
| games with both line and ratings | 0/11 | **5/11** |

The 2 unresolved (`San Diego St Aztecs`, `Albany Great Danes`) had **already
tipped** when the fix landed, so the pregame filter correctly skips them. The 3

### The actual constraint: NCAAB splits were scraped for months and never archived

`line_snapshot` before tonight:

```
MLB 16,882 . NCAAF 2,179 . NFL 1,191 . NBA 847 . NHL 732 . NCAAB 0
```

Zero, on the sport with the most games. Not for want of data - `ncaab_pipeline.yml`
already scrapes (`pull_scoresandodds --sport NCAAB`) and aggregates
(`splits_v2_pipeline --sport NCAAB`), and context rows carry the blobs now.

Two reasons, and the first is the memorable one: `write_line_snapshot.CTX_TABLE`
had no `'NCAAB'` key while its `_ML_COLS` map - the part needing real
verification - has carried NCAAB for months. **The hard half was wired and the
easy half was not.** With the key missing, `run()` prints "no ctx table
registered - skip" and returns 0, indistinguishable from a night with no games.
Second: no workflow ever invoked it for NCAAB, while all five other sports have
an explicit call.

The cost beyond missing history: `align_status_common.load_public_splits_snapshot`
**READS** `line_snapshot` at `source='so'`, so NCAAB alignment has been computed
with no public-splits vote at all. The chain in `compute_align_status_nba.py`'s
docstring runs the opposite way from how it reads - line_snapshot is the SOURCE
and the context blob is derived from it - so an empty table starves the consumer
rather than merely failing to record.

Fixed in `f7b19134`: registered the table, invoked the writer from
`ncaab_pipeline.yml` immediately after the splits aggregator and well before
`generate_ncaab_game_reads.py`, because a splits step landing after the reads
loses the scorer its vote even on runs that succeed. Verified by parsing the
YAML - splits (step 21) -> line_snapshot (22) -> reads (33).

Also fixed the hardcoded ET offset in `write_line_snapshot.py` and
`compute_align_status_ncaab.py`. The NBA and NHL copies were moved to a real
ZoneInfo on 11-10 and the NCAAB one was left on `(now_utc - 4h)` - the exact
shape of `feedback_fix_every_writer_to_the_table`. And asserted the invariant the
fix rests on, parsed from source: every sport in `CTX_TABLE` has an `_ML_COLS`
mapping, so adding a sport to one map and not the other fails loudly.

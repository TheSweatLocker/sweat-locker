## 2. Description (4,000 char max)

```
The Sweat Locker turns sports betting into a data problem.

We built the tools we wished existed: a daily edge machine that reads the market the way sharp bettors do, filters what the models agree on, and hands you the plays that survive.

DAILY COVERAGE ACROSS EVERY MAJOR SPORT

• NFL — spreads, totals, moneylines, player props, matchup context
• NBA — game-by-game reads, Elo ratings, line movement tracked
• MLB — full season, sides + totals + props + Player of the Day
• NCAAF — every FBS game, efficiency ratings, sharp-money divergence
• NCAAB — full season coverage starting Nov 3
• NHL — full slate, expected goals, goalie context
• UFC — fight-by-fight, calibrated fighter probabilities

THE SWEAT CARD
Our daily "these are the plays" list. Capped at 4 sides + 3–5 props. Only conviction plays make it. If the models don't agree, it doesn't ship.

THE STEAM ROOM
Where you watch the market move. The Split (bets vs. handle), The Ladder (single-play parlays), The Sharp (a disciplined sharp-money list with a higher bar than the daily card), and The Ledger of graded parlays and teasers.

JERRY, OUR SYNTHESIS ENGINE
Every game and every prop gets a plain-English read: what the model saw, what the market's telling you, and where the edge is — or isn't. No hedging, no filler.

VAULT MATCH
Pattern recognition on historic games. When today's matchup matches a proven ATS or Over/Under pattern from the archive, we surface it as an alternate edge.

MONEYFLOW
Public bets % vs. sharp handle %, averaged across split sources. When Vegas is fading the public, we tell you.

RECEIPTS
Every pick we've ever surfaced — graded nightly, rolled up by sport and tier. No cherry-picking, no revisionism. If it hit the app, it's in the record.

EXTERNAL PICKS
Sharp handicapper releases from across the industry, aggregated and tracked with the same rigor as our own plays.

PICK OF THE DAY
Highest conviction across every sport, every surface. One play. Every day.

NOT A SPORTSBOOK
We don't accept wagers. We don't take a rake. We build the analytics — you use them however you want, at whatever book you already use. No sportsbook links, no affiliate junk.

MEMBERSHIP
7-day free trial, then $14.99/month or $119.99/year. Cancel anytime from your Apple ID subscriptions.

More Data, Less Sweat.

—

For informational and entertainment purposes only. The Sweat Locker does not accept or place wagers, is not affiliated with any sportsbook, and does not guarantee outcomes. Must be 18+ where applicable. If gambling is not fun, call 1-800-GAMBLER.

Terms of Use: https://thesweatlocker.com/terms
Privacy Policy: https://thesweatlocker.com/privacy
```

**Notes:** Lead with the value prop, then coverage, then features, then the not-a-sportsbook clarifier (critical for Apple review), then pricing. Ends with the compliance disclaimer per Apple guideline 5.3 (real-money gaming) — even though we're informational only, calling it out reduces reviewer friction.

## 9. Notes (4,000 char max — critical for approval)

```
WHAT THE APP IS
Sports betting analytics and editorial content — data-driven analysis, statistical models, and plain-English reads on daily games and player props across NFL, NBA, MLB, NCAAF, NCAAB, NHL, and UFC. Informational sports content per Apple guideline 5.3; no real-money gaming.

WHAT THE APP IS NOT
Not a sportsbook. Does not accept, facilitate, or process wagers. Not affiliated with any sportsbook operator. No sportsbook links, no affiliate redirects, no bet-placement flows.

NO LOGIN REQUIRED
No auth system. No username, password, or signup screen — users open the app and see content immediately. Please leave the sign-in requirement UNCHECKED for review.

HOW TO REVIEW

The app is subscription-gated. Please complete a sandbox purchase to see the full product. Everything unlocks with one subscription — no tiered access, no add-ons.

WHAT A NEW USER SEES BEFORE SUBSCRIBING (free / limited state)

1. Home screen — the Pick of the Day panel is fully visible for free (headline pick, matchup, conviction score, Jerry's narrative). This is intentional: reviewers and prospective users get a real example of the product's output before hitting the wall. Directly below it, a locked "🔒 REST OF TODAY'S CARD" panel replaces the rest of the curated list, with a CTA button that reads "Start 7-Day Free Trial." Tapping opens the subscription sheet.

2. Games tab (bottom nav) — today's game slate is visible for every active sport (matchups, times, market prices). Per-game pick chips (tier labels, Jerry snippet previews) are locked with a padlock; tapping any lock opens the subscription sheet.

3. Game Detail (tap any game) — matchup, teams, and market prices are visible. Below that, a single "🔒 SWEAT LOCKER PRO — Unlock the full analysis" panel lists every locked section. Tapping opens the subscription sheet.

4. Jerry tab and Steam Room tab — fully subscription-gated. Each shows a paywall preview panel describing what's inside. There is no free view of these two tabs.

STARTING THE SANDBOX PURCHASE

5. Tap any lock icon or any "Start 7-Day Free Trial" CTA. The Apple subscription sheet appears with two products:
   - Monthly: $14.99/mo, auto-renewing, 7-day free trial for new subscribers
   - Annual: $119.99/yr, auto-renewing, 7-day free trial for new subscribers
6. Complete the purchase using your Apple sandbox tester account (Settings → Developer → Sandbox Apple Account on the test device). No in-app sign-in is needed — Apple IAP handles the transaction end-to-end.

AFTER SUBSCRIBING (full product unlocked)

7. Home — full Sweat Card appears in place of the locked panel: today's curated plays with tier chips (PRIME / STRONG / LEAN / READ), Dawg of the Day, yesterday's recap.
8. Games tab — Jerry snippets and tier chips render on every game card. Game Detail unlocks the full analytical stack (Jerry read, predicted score, MoneyFlow, handicappers, situational records, team stats, line movement, splits, Vault Match).
9. Jerry tab — every game's full read plus every player prop read, across all active sports.
10. Steam Room tab — all four sub-tabs: The Split, The Ladder, The Sharp, The Ledger.

DATA + COMPLIANCE
- Data sourced from public sports APIs, publicly available stats, and public betting market data (odds APIs, public splits aggregators). No scraping of proprietary/paywalled data.
- No user-generated content, no social features, no chat, no location tracking.
- Legal-betting-age guidance in FAQ + Terms ("legal betting age in your jurisdiction, varies 18-21 by state"). 1-800-GAMBLER disclaimer in FAQ + Terms.
- No sportsbook links anywhere in the app.
- Payments and cancellations flow through Apple ID only — no external payment processing. Cancel anytime.
- Privacy policy: https://thesweatlocker.com/privacy
- Terms of service: https://thesweatlocker.com/terms

Happy to walk through any part of the app if you need additional context — please contact us at the email above. Thank you for your time.
```

**Notes:** Front-loaded with "not a sportsbook" because this is the #1 rejection risk for a sports betting analytics app. Walks the reviewer through a review flow so they don't hunt for premium features. Explicit note on age/compliance/no-links reinforces Apple guideline 5.3 posture.

**Rule:** the daily ladder pick and the public 5-card are two distinct surfaces. The ladder pick must NEVER appear on the public 5-card.

**Why:** [reasoning the user has signaled, even if not stated each time]
- Ladder is a focused, bankroll-rolling single play — it's its own surface
- Duplicating it on the public card dilutes both: ladder becomes "just another pick," public card loses a slot for breadth
- Public 5 is meant for variety + slate coverage
- If the ladder pick busts AND it was on the public card, you took two hits on one outcome (correlated downside)

**How to apply:**
- When assembling the daily card, pick the ladder FIRST as a dedicated single play
- Then build a 5-card from the REMAINING playable picks
- Never list the ladder pick anywhere in the public 5
- If the public 5 has fewer than 5 strong candidates after removing the ladder pick, surface fewer (e.g. 4-card) rather than duplicating

When doing the morning audit (or any "look at yesterday + today" recap), the ML/Side resolver gets equal billing with Totals and Props. Three sections, not two.

The failure pattern:
- I focused on totals + props because those are where most public picks live
- I de-weighted sides because the live record was rocky early (SIDE STRONG was 0-2 then 0-5 in the first week of live tracking)
- User called this out on 6/16 — sides went 7-0 (5 STRONG + 2 LIGHT) on 6/15 and I didn't mention it in the audit

How to apply:
- Morning audit ALWAYS has three sections: Totals, Sides, Props
- For each: yesterday's tier grade + today's picks
- Even when sides have been losing, show the data — that's the framework working transparently
- Don't bury sides in honorable mentions or "by the way" — they're co-equal to totals

When the side resolver IS hitting (like the 6/15 7-0 sweep), that's massive credibility content the user should know about for picks AND for social posts. The data backs it.

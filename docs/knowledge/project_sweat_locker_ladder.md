The Sweat Locker Ladder is a daily compounding ladder: one single highest-conviction play per day. Followers stake the rung, if it wins, they roll the full payout into the next day's rung. Continues until either (a) a rung loses (ladder dies) or (b) the user hits a pre-declared target number of rungs.

**Why:** Established 2026-06-09 — user clarified after I mis-framed the ladder as a 3-leg same-day play with anchor + extras + receipt. The actual product is sequential, not horizontal.

**How to apply:**
- One play per ladder post. Not multiple legs.
- Pick the single highest-confidence play of the slate (cohort net edge + model unanimity + sample size)
- For ladder picks, prioritize model unanimity over net-cohort-edge — a dissenting model introduces ladder-killer risk that doesn't exist for a one-off play
- Frame social posts as "Step #N" not "Anchor / Extras / Receipt"
- Receipts and supplemental cohort findings can still be shared, but as separate content, not as ladder legs
